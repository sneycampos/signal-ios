Good questions

Before you ask.

When can I download Mesh Planner?

The app is currently pending App Store review. A download link will appear here when it is available.

Is the app free?

Yes. Wi-Fi Mesh Planner: LiDAR Scan is free.

Do I need a device with LiDAR?

LiDAR room scanning requires a LiDAR-equipped iPhone or iPad. Check that your device has a LiDAR scanner before using the scanning feature.

Which Wi-Fi bands can I explore?

Mesh Planner supports 2.4 GHz, 5 GHz and 6 GHz bands, with room-by-room heatmaps, a signal inspector and mesh placement planning.

Are my scans or measurements uploaded?

No. Scans and projects stay on your device. They leave it only when you choose to export or share them. The app has no accounts, analytics or advertising. Read the privacy policy.

How can I report a problem?

Email sneycampos@gmail.com or open a GitHub issue. Tell us your device model, iOS version, what you expected, and the steps that led to the problem.
