FAQ

Which devices support room scanning? iPhone and iPad models with LiDAR (Pro models). Other devices can use the demo apartment and manual editing.

Are heatmaps exact? They are estimates from a propagation model using wall materials; use calibration in the app to match real measurements.

Is my data uploaded? No. Everything stays on your device.
