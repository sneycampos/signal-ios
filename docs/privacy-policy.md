# Privacy Policy — Signal

_Last updated: 15 October 2026_

Signal ("the app") is developed by Silvio Ney.

**We do not collect any data.** The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs, and it makes no network requests.

- **Camera and LiDAR:** used only on your device to scan rooms (Apple RoomPlan) and show the AR walkthrough. Images and scans are not uploaded anywhere.
- **Projects:** floor plans, equipment and measurements are stored locally in the app's Documents folder. They leave your device only if you export or share them yourself.
- **Diagnostics:** an optional diagnostics text you can copy or share manually; it is never sent automatically.

Children: the app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy will be published on this page.

Contact: open an issue at https://github.com/sneycampos/signal-ios/issues or email sneycampos@gmail.com.

# Política de Privacidade — Signal (pt-PT)

**Não recolhemos quaisquer dados.** A app não tem conta, análise, publicidade nem SDKs de terceiros, e não faz pedidos de rede. A câmara e o LiDAR são usados apenas no dispositivo para digitalizar divisões e para a vista em realidade aumentada. Os projetos ficam guardados localmente e só saem do dispositivo se os exportar ou partilhar.
