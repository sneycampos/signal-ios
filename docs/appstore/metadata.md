## App Review notes

Signal uses Apple RoomPlan, which requires a LiDAR scanner (iPhone 12 Pro or later Pro models, iPad Pro 2020+). On devices without LiDAR the scan button reports that the device is unsupported; the reviewer can still open the built-in demo apartment from the home screen to try the heatmap, equipment placement, optimizer and report without scanning. The camera is used only for the room scan and AR walkthrough; nothing is uploaded. No login is required.
